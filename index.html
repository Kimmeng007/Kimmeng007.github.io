Profile

I'm a Master's student in Data Science at Université Paris-Saclay, with a double engineering degree in Applied Mathematics at ENSIIE, and prior engineering training in Data Science at the Institut de Technologie du Cambodge. My work sits at the intersection of computer vision, deep learning, and applied statistics — building models with PyTorch that hold up outside the notebook.

Recent work spans fine-tuning YOLO detectors and semantic segmentation models for flooded environments, synthetic multi-modal dataset generation in Unreal Engine, CNN–LSTM architectures for spatio-temporal video tasks, and gradient-boosted models for tabular and graph-structured data. I care about pipelines that are reproducible end to end — data, training, and evaluation — not just leaderboard scores.
